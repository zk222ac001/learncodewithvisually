Privacy information

Last updated: 12 October 2026. This notice is under review. The site operator must confirm the complete operational, legal and advertising disclosures before launching new services.

Local learning progress

Some interactive lessons store language preferences, quiz attempts, and completion markers in your browser's local storage so your progress can be remembered on the same device. You can clear this information through your browser's settings for site data.

Third-party code editors

Some programming exercises open OneCompiler, an external code-editing platform. If you choose to open it, the information and code you submit to that service are governed by its own policies. Avoid entering personal or confidential information in examples.

Website delivery and security providers

Public learning pages are published with GitHub Pages. The optional AI Tutor backend uses Cloudflare Workers, Workers AI and Turnstile. These providers may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and security signals, when delivering or protecting their services. The operator must review actual hosting, DNS, analytics and log settings and document applicable provider terms and retention periods.

Optional AI Tutor and student questions

When AI Tutor is enabled and you choose an AI action, your question (if any), the selected C lesson step and a verification token are sent to the Cloudflare Worker. The Worker verifies the Turnstile challenge with Cloudflare and sends the trusted lesson context and question to Cloudflare Workers AI for an AI-generated explanation or hint. Cloudflare also processes network/security metadata needed for these requests, including an IP address for temporary rate limiting.

The current application code does not deliberately store student questions, AI responses or IP addresses in its own database. A shared Cloudflare Durable Object holds only the UTC day and the number of AI requests reserved that day. Provider-operated security, diagnostic or service logs may be subject to separate retention settings; their exact configuration must be checked by the operator. Please do not submit names, email addresses, passwords, health information or other personal or confidential data in an AI question.

AI responses may be inaccurate and should be checked against the deterministic visualization. The AI Tutor is optional: programming tutorials and visualizations are available without using it. Cloudflare states that Workers AI customer content is not used to train or improve models or services without explicit consent; see Cloudflare Workers AI data usage and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Before public activation, the operator must confirm the applicable legal basis, responsibility for personal data, provider agreements, retention and deletion procedures, whether minors may use AI Tutor, and relevant rights/consent disclosures. Email the address below to ask about your information.
